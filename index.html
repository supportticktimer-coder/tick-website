PRIVACY POLICY

Effective date: November 22, 2026

TICK is a free interval workout timer for iOS. This privacy policy describes the current version of the app.

1. Data collection

TICK does not require an account and does not intentionally collect personal information through the app. Workout timer functions run locally on your device.

2. Analytics and advertising

The current version of TICK does not include advertising, advertising trackers or third-party analytics services.

3. Microphone and audio

TICK provides workout sound cues and voice announcements. TICK does not record microphone audio or upload audio recordings.

4. Support emails

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the information you choose to provide. We use this information to respond to your request.

5. Third-party services

TICK uses software components to provide its app functionality. The app does not intentionally transmit workout data to external analytics or advertising services.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy if the app's functionality or data practices change. Any future advertising features will be reflected in an updated policy.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact: support.ticktimer@gmail.com
